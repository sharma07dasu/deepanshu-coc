How We're Organized

Three departments, one mission.

Strategic Advisory, Research & Editorial, and Public Relations & Branding — each with its own focus, working toward the same case-solving culture.

Strategic Advisory

Corporate Live Projects

Work directly with startups, SMEs, and corporates on business-critical problems — market entry strategies, competitor analysis, business model validation.

Weekly Case Study Discussion

Analyze real-world business scenarios weekly to sharpen problem-solving, critical thinking, and strategic decision-making.

Case Prep & Guesstimates

Internal upskilling for consulting interviews — weekly case practice, guesstimate drills, and the Strategy Lab Prep Handbook.

CoC Annual Casebook

Our flagship resource: curated consulting frameworks, guesstimate approaches, and interview prep tools.

Research and Editorial

Industry Intelligence

Tracking trends across FMCG, FinTech, and EdTech into in-depth, actionable reports.

Behind The Brands

Breaking down real business successes and failures — like the Zomato IPO or Paytm crash — for lessons future leaders can use.

Policy & Market Analysis

Evaluating how government policy and budgets shape industries, bridging economics with business outcomes.

Collaborative Research

Co-authoring papers with faculty and senior researchers, backed by data-driven analysis.

Public Relations and Branding

Brainstorming

Shaping creative, innovative ideas that lift engagement across our projects and initiatives.

Visually Appealing Content

Producing captivating content for our media handles using design tools and multimedia.

Events & Activities

Ensuring seamless technical operations during events, for a hassle-free experience.

Creative Campaigns

Planning and executing campaigns that promote our events, initiatives, and the club itself.
