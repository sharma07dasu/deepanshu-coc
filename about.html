Cases Over Coffee, Global

Founded in 2020 and based out of Singapore, Cases Over Coffee was built to create a global platform for knowledge-sharing and skill-building in consulting, case-solving, and beyond.

Our partners, associates, and experts come from globally renowned institutions — Wharton, Stanford, INSEAD, Cornell, Johns Hopkins, McGill, Tufts, ISB, and the IIMs — alongside corporates including McKinsey, Bain, BCG, Dalberg, Goldman Sachs, Google, and Disney.
